## Selected work

Lead engineer on the Safir-M digital pressure sensor at Manometer-Kharkiv. Accuracy 0.1%, outputs 4–20 mA and 0–5 mA, HART, color OLED. Architecture of four MSP430 microcontrollers. Fixed- and floating-point math rewritten by hand to fit limited flash, cut power use, and keep response under 0.1 s, including on a range reduced to 1/10. Passed HART certification. Patent UA 102315. In serial production at the Merefa plant since 2013.

Also designed electronics and firmware for earlier digital pressure sensors from the same plant, a USB-HART modem, and industrial controllers with CAN, RS-485, and Ethernet.

## Skills
